Cappadocia Blue Tour

  • StopsOrtahisar, Keşlik, Sobesos, Soğanlı, Mazı
  • DurationFull day
  • WhatsApp

The quiet side of Cappadocia.

The quiet south: lavender views, the frescoed Keşlik Monastery, Roman mosaics at Sobesos, Soğanlı Valley and Mazı underground city.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Lavender Panorama (Ortahisar)

    Lavender fields with Ortahisar Castle behind them.

  2. 02

    Keşlik Monastery

    A quiet rock-carved monastery with frescoes.

  3. 03

    Sobesos ancient city

    A Roman town with well-preserved mosaics and the ruins of a bathhouse.

  4. 04

    Soğanlı Valley

    A walk among fairy chimneys and rock churches.

  5. 05

    Lunch

    Turkish cuisine with local dishes.

  6. 06

    Mazı underground city

    A hidden underground world once used by locals for protection.

  7. 07

    Carpet shop

    See how traditional Turkish carpets are woven.

What's included

Included

  • Professional guide
  • Entrance fees to all sites
  • Lunch during the tour
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off

Not included

  • Drinks at lunch

Good to know

  • A lesser-known southern route, away from the crowds.
  • There is a walk in Soğanlı, so wear comfortable shoes.
