Balloons

Pamukkale Hot Air Balloon Ride

  • PlacePamukkale, Denizli
  • TimeSunrise
  • WhatsApp

White terraces, first light.

A second balloon experience after Cappadocia: a sunrise flight over the white travertines of Pamukkale and the ancient city of Hierapolis.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Pickup before sunrise

    We pick you up from your hotel in Pamukkale or Karahayıt.

  2. 02

    Sunrise flight

    Fly for 60-75 minutes over the travertines, the ancient city of Hierapolis and Cleopatra's Pool.

  3. 03

    Celebration

    After a soft landing, a champagne toast and a personalised flight certificate.

  4. 04

    Return

    You are usually back at your hotel in time for breakfast.

What's included

Included

  • Hotel transfer from Pamukkale or Karahayıt
  • 60-75 minute balloon flight (weather dependent)
  • Licensed pilot and flight crew
  • Full passenger insurance
  • Post-flight champagne toast
  • Personalised flight certificate

Not included

  • Meals not mentioned in the programme
  • Professional photos and video (optional, for purchase)
  • Transfers from cities outside Pamukkale (can be arranged at extra cost)
  • Personal expenses and tips

Good to know

  • The whole experience takes 2-2.5 hours, about 1 hour of it in the air.
  • Flights always take place at sunrise, when the air is calmest, and depend on the weather.
  • Children under 6 may not be allowed to fly. If you are pregnant, please ask us first.
  • Mornings can be chilly: wear comfortable shoes and layers.
