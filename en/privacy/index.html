Privacy and cookies

Who we are

This website belongs to Cappadocia Vista Tours (TÜRSAB 17102, Göreme, Nevşehir, Türkiye). For any question about your personal information, write to us at cappavistatours@gmail.com.

When you browse the site

You do not need to give a name, an email address or sign up to browse the site. The site sits on a hosting service (currently GitHub Pages). As with every website, that service sees your IP address in order to send you the pages.

The form and WhatsApp

When you press the button on the form, WhatsApp opens with a ready message containing what you typed (your name, date, number of people, the tours you are interested in). You send the message yourself. At the moment you press the button, the same details are also saved in our own booking panel so that we can follow up your request. Once you send the WhatsApp message, we see your phone number, your WhatsApp name and what you wrote. We use these to answer you and to arrange your booking. WhatsApp and Instagram have their own privacy rules.

Cookies and visit statistics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit the site and which pages are viewed. This only runs if you choose “Allow”. If you allow it, Google places cookies in your browser (their names start with _ga) and visit information goes to Google. We do not use advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie choice” link at the bottom of every page. If you choose “No thanks”, these cookies are deleted.

Our own visit statistics

To see how the site is used, a short visit record also goes to our own panel: the language of the page, which website or advertisement you came from (only the name of the site), your type of device (phone, tablet or computer), your time zone (to estimate the country), how far down the page you looked, how long you stayed, and whether you pressed the WhatsApp button or the form button. This does not use cookies and saves nothing on your device. Your IP address is not stored. To link the pages you open on the same day, your IP address and browser name are mixed with a random value that changes every day, in a way that cannot be reversed. The next day we cannot recognise you. These records are deleted after 400 days. This information does not go to Google. It stays in our panel on a server we rent.

What is kept on your device

The language you pick and your cookie choice are kept in your browser, so you are not asked again on every visit. These are not cookies and they stay on your device.

Prices

To show current prices, your browser reads the public price list on our sister site travel-cappadocia.com. No cookies or form details are sent to that site while it does so. As with any connection, your IP address is visible.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. These rights are set out in Türkiye's data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) and, for visitors from the EU, in the GDPR. Writing to cappavistatours@gmail.com is enough.
