Cappadocia Turkish Bath (Hamam)

  • ServiceScrub and foam massage
  • WhatsApp

Warm marble at the end of the day.

Warm marble under the dome, a scrub and a foam massage. The best way to rest after a long day of touring.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Hotel pickup

    We pick you up from your hotel in Göreme.

  2. 02

    Getting ready

    Wrap yourself in the cotton peştemal your attendant gives you. Your belongings stay in a secure locker.

  3. 03

    Scrub and foam massage

    A kese scrub and a soft foam massage.

  4. 04

    Oil massage

    Relax with an oil massage, as light or strong as you like.

What's included

Included

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off within Göreme
  • Scrub and foam massage
  • Peştemal
  • Secure locker for your belongings

Good to know

  • Hotel transfers are only valid within Göreme. If you stay outside Göreme, ask us about the transfer.
  • The hamam is an immaculate, elegant underground space.
