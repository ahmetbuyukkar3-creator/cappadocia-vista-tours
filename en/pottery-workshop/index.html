Cappadocia Pottery Workshop

  • PlaceAvanos
  • LevelNo experience needed
  • WhatsApp

The clay of the Red River in your hands.

In Avanos, home of a pottery tradition thousands of years old, shape your own pot on the wheel next to a master.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Hotel pickup

    A shuttle picks you up from your hotel or any place you prefer.

  2. 02

    Meet the master

    A master potter in Avanos explains this age-old craft.

  3. 03

    Your turn at the wheel

    Shape your own pot for 10-15 minutes.

  4. 04

    Workshop and return

    Browse the workshop's pieces, then head back to your hotel.

What's included

Included

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off
  • Pottery making (10-15 minutes)
  • Soft drinks
  • Vehicle, English-speaking driver and fuel

Good to know

  • The programme takes about 20 minutes.
  • Avanos once had hundreds of potters; the craft is still alive today.
