Cappadocia Camel Riding Tour

  • ValleysRed, Rose, Meskendir
  • DurationAbout 2 hours
  • WhatsApp

Like a caravan at sunset.

A short ride on calm camels along the sandy ridges of the valley, with a photo stop as the sun sets behind the fairy chimneys.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Hotel pickup

    We pick you up from your hotel to start the tour.

  2. 02

    Red Valley

    The first part of the ride among bright red rocks.

  3. 03

    Rose Valley

    A peaceful valley known for its pinkish rocks.

  4. 04

    Meskendir Valley

    A quieter route surrounded by tall rock formations.

  5. 05

    Back to the hotel

    At the end of the tour we take you back to your hotel.

What's included

Included

  • Camel ride through the Red, Rose and Meskendir valleys
  • Professional guide
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off
  • Insurance during the tour

Not included

  • Drinks during the tour

Good to know

  • There is a 120 kg weight limit.
  • The tour takes about two hours.
