Cappadocia Balloon and Day Tour

  • OptionsBalloon + Mix · Balloon + Green
  • DurationFull day
  • WhatsApp

Sky in the morning, valleys in the afternoon.

A balloon flight at dawn, then a Mix or Green tour the same day. See Cappadocia from the air and from the ground in one day.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Balloon at dawn

    We pick you up at least an hour before the flight. After a light breakfast and the pilot's briefing, a 60-minute flight.

  2. 02

    Celebration

    Non-alcoholic champagne and a flight certificate after landing.

  3. 03

    Day tour

    Your chosen route: Mix (Göreme Panorama, Uçhisar, Pigeon Valley, an underground city, Devrent, Paşabağ, Zelve, Avanos) or Green (Ihlara, riverside lunch in Belisırma, Selime).

  4. 04

    Return

    The tour runs from 09:30 to 18:00, and you are dropped at your hotel in the evening.

What's included

Included

  • Hotel pickup and return
  • Light breakfast before the flight
  • Pilot's briefing
  • Celebration and flight certificate
  • Full insurance
  • Guided day tour
  • Lunch
  • Museum entrance fees

Good to know

  • Balloons in Cappadocia share the same standard and carry 24-28 people.
  • If the weather does not allow it, the flight can be moved to another day.
  • Not suitable for pregnant travellers, wheelchair users or people with serious heart conditions.
  • Please send your hotel details the day before the flight.
