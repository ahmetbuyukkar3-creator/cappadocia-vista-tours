Horseback Riding in Cappadocia

  • LevelBeginner friendly
  • Best timeSunset
  • WhatsApp

In the land of beautiful horses, on horseback.

Cappadocia takes its name from "the land of beautiful horses". On our calm horses along the narrow paths of the Rose Valley, an easy ride even for first-timers.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Meet your horse

    You meet your horse and learn the basics of mounting and holding the reins.

  2. 02

    Into the valley

    With a guide, in single file, onto the trails of the Rose Valley. The pace is a walk.

  3. 03

    When the light changes

    The light shifts as you ride between the fairy chimneys. The best time is sunset.

What's included

Included

  • Hotel transfer
  • Small group with an experienced guide

Good to know

  • Wear long trousers and closed shoes.
  • If you have never ridden before, tell the guide before you start.
  • Ask us about children and the weight limit.
  • Route: Swords Valley, Red Valley, Rose Valley and Çavuşin village, with views of Uçhisar Castle and Göreme.
  • For quieter valleys, daytime rides are also a good choice.
