At dawn, the sky is yours.

Sunrise over the fairy chimneys. Small baskets, experienced pilots and the same care every morning.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Pick-up before dawn

    We collect you from your hotel and drive to the launch site together.

  2. 02

    Breakfast and preparation

    A light breakfast while the balloon is inflated. Your pilot gives a short safety briefing before take-off.

  3. 03

    The flight

    You drift above the valleys and fairy chimneys as the sun comes up. The route follows that morning's wind.

  4. 04

    Landing and certificate

    After landing you receive your flight certificate and we take you back to your hotel.

What's included

Included

  • Hotel pickup and return
  • Light breakfast before the flight
  • Transfer to the launch site
  • Pre-flight briefing by the pilot
  • Certificate of participation
  • Full insurance

Good to know

  • Balloons fly only with the permission the Civil Aviation authority gives that morning. The decision depends on the weather and is made shortly before dawn.
  • Mornings are cold. Dress in layers and wear flat, closed shoes.
  • You stand in the basket for the whole flight.
  • Plan the balloon for the first morning of your trip: if the weather does not allow it, it can be moved to another morning.
  • Economy, comfort and private flights are available. In comfort class there are at most 24 or 28 people in the basket.
  • Children under 5 cannot fly; children under 16 fly with an adult.
  • Pregnant guests, people who recently had major surgery and people with serious heart conditions cannot fly.
  • Please send your hotel details the day before the flight. Pickup time depends on the sunrise.
  • Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the flight are fully refunded.
