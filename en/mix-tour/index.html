Cappadocia Mix Tour

  • StopsPaşabağ, Zelve, Kaymaklı, Avanos, Uçhisar
  • DurationFull day
  • WhatsApp

The best of Red and Green.

The favourite stops in one day: Paşabağ, Zelve, Kaymaklı Underground City, pottery in Avanos and Uçhisar Castle.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Paşabağ

    The day starts with its unique fairy chimneys.

  2. 02

    Zelve and Devrent

    Cave homes and churches carved into the rock, and rocks that spark the imagination.

  3. 03

    Lunch

    Traditional Cappadocian dishes at a local restaurant.

  4. 04

    Kaymaklı Underground City

    An underground city where people once took shelter.

  5. 05

    Pottery in Avanos

    Watch pottery being made and try it yourself.

  6. 06

    Love Valley and Uçhisar Castle

    The viewpoints of the day.

  7. 07

    Leather shop

    The day ends at a shop of traditionally crafted leather goods.

What's included

Included

  • Transport in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle
  • All entrance fees, including Kaymaklı Underground City
  • Lunch at a local restaurant
  • Pottery-making experience

  • Tips for the guide and driver
