Balloons

Soğanlı Valley Hot Air Balloon Ride

  • PlaceSoğanlı Valley
  • ClassComfort
  • WhatsApp

A sky away from the crowds.

A calm flight with only a few balloons over the green Soğanlı Valley, south of Göreme. Rock-cut churches and a quiet valley below you.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Hotel pickup

    We pick you up from your hotel or any place you prefer at least one hour before the flight.

  2. 02

    Breakfast and briefing

    A light breakfast at the launch site, then a pre-flight briefing by an experienced pilot.

  3. 03

    Flight over Soğanlı

    The flight lasts 1 to 1.5 hours. The landing spot depends on the wind, usually near the valley.

  4. 04

    Celebration and return

    Champagne toast and medal after landing. A shuttle takes you back to your hotel.

What's included

Included

  • Hotel pickup and return
  • Light breakfast before the flight
  • Transfer to the launch site
  • Pre-flight briefing by the pilot
  • Champagne toast and gold medal
  • Full insurance

Good to know

  • Balloons only fly with that morning's flight permit. If the weather does not allow it, the flight moves to another morning.
  • Mornings are cool. Dress in layers and wear comfortable closed shoes.
