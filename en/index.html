FAQ

Good questions.

How much do the tours cost?

Prices depend on the tour, the date and the number of people. Write to us on WhatsApp and we will give you an exact price.

Do you pick up from hotels?

On most tours, yes. The pick-up time depends on the tour; just send us the name of your hotel.

What if the balloon flight is cancelled due to weather?

Balloons only fly with that morning's permission. If the weather does not allow it, the flight can move to another morning. That is why we suggest booking the balloon for the first morning of your trip.

Can children fly in the balloon?

Children under 5 cannot fly. Under-16s fly with an adult.

What is the difference between the Red, Green and Blue Tours?

The Red Tour covers the northern fairy chimneys, Zelve and Uçhisar. The Green Tour goes to an underground city and Ihlara Valley. The Blue Tour is a quieter southern route. The Mix Tour brings together the favourite stops of the Red and Green Tours in one day.

What should I wear?

Mornings are cool. Dress in layers and wear flat, closed shoes. Day tours involve plenty of walking.

Are you a licensed agency?

Yes. Our TÜRSAB licence number is 17102.
