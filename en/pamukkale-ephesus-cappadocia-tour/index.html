Pamukkale, Ephesus and Cappadocia Tour

  • Duration4 days 3 nights
  • StopsCappadocia, Pamukkale, Ephesus
  • WhatsApp

Three treasures of Türkiye.

In 4 days and 3 nights: the valleys of Cappadocia, the white terraces of Pamukkale and the ancient city of Ephesus. Transport and stays are planned.

How the tour goes

  1. 01

    Day 1: Pamukkale

    Walk barefoot on the travertines, then Hierapolis, the Temple of Apollo and the theatre. After lunch, to your hotel in Kuşadası.

  2. 02

    Day 2: Ephesus

    The Library of Celsus, the Great Theatre, the Temple of Hadrian, the House of the Virgin Mary and the Temple of Artemis.

  3. 03

    Day 3: Cappadocia

    Flight from Izmir to Cappadocia. Devrent, Paşabağ, Avanos and Göreme Open-Air Museum. Night in a cave hotel.

  4. 04

    Day 4: Southern Cappadocia and return

    A guided southern tour after breakfast, evening flight to Istanbul.

What's included

Included

  • 3 nights of hotels in Kuşadası and Cappadocia
  • Domestic flights between Istanbul, Denizli, Izmir and Cappadocia
  • Airport transfers in air-conditioned, non-smoking vehicles
  • Daily breakfast at the hotel
  • Lunch on tour days
  • Licensed English-speaking guide
  • Entrance fees to all scheduled museums and sites
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off on every tour day
  • Vegetarian and vegan options on request

Not included

  • International flights
  • Turkish visa (if needed)
  • Personal expenses and optional activities
  • Drinks at lunch
  • Dinners
  • Tips for guides and drivers

Good to know

  • A swim in Cleopatra's Pool in Pamukkale is optional.
  • In Cappadocia you stay overnight in a cave hotel.
